# DreamSavant plugin privacy notice

Updated October 26, 2026. This notice applies to the downloadable DreamSavant
skill and plugin, not to the account-based DreamSavant application.

This release contains instructions and a logo only. It has no MCP server,
executable scripts, authentication, telemetry, or storage. It does not send
dreams or conversations to DreamSavant, read a DreamSavant account, or save
journal entries. Claude processes the text you supply under your Claude
account's terms, settings and privacy policies. We cannot specify Anthropic's
retention period on your behalf.

Dream content can be personal or sensitive. Share only material you want
processed in Claude. The skill does not request names, passwords, tokens or
identifying information. It is intended for a general adult audience and is
not designed for children, clinical diagnosis, or medical treatment.

The skill offers optional public DreamSavant links. Links use fixed UTM
attribution parameters identifying Claude as the referral source; they contain
no dream text or personal identifiers. If you choose to open a link, the
DreamSavant website and hosting providers process normal request information
under https://dreamsavant.com/privacy. Browsing public pages at your request
uses Claude's browsing tools and their applicable data policies, not a hidden
plugin connection. The plugin defines no additional service or retention period.

Support: support@dreamsavant.com. Privacy: privacy@dreamsavant.com.
